# Seven motions design

## World and palette
Seven complete rooms, each inheriting the exact guide palette. First room is #F4F1EA graph paper and #2F5BFF pen, with darker #203DBA interface text for legibility. Other rooms use near-black/red/cobalt/cream, phosphor green/black, four-colour green pixels, pink/teal paper, sepia/red-chalk parchment, and indigo/magenta on white. The cream palette is intentional source fidelity, not a generic neutral theme.

## Typography
Self-hosted Anton gives the gallery and graphic films condensed poster lettering. Verdana is reserved for readable interface/body copy; monospace is used for terminal glyphs and film measurements, Georgia for the codex. Main display scale 32–76px; functional copy 12px minimum, body 13–14px. Film labels are generally 60–80px at source resolution; ASCII glyph cells intentionally smaller.

## Composition
One vertical film dominates each full-screen room. Desktop copy sits low at left with technique notes right; mobile brings heading/story above a tall film and transport. A fixed numbered room index shows sequence and permits direct navigation. No card grid. Room palette extends behind the film, with its vertical texture boundary intentionally visible.

## Interaction
Video is the efficient playback view of deterministic Canvas source. Play/pause, sound toggle and seek are explicit. Only the visible room autoplays, muted; reduced-motion mode remains on the poster until an explicit play. Sound is never enabled automatically; starting sound mutes other films. Keyboard focus is high-contrast and visible. Link underlines, selection and scrollbar colours belong to the same palette.

## Finish review
Reviewed original per-style contact sheets plus 1440px and 390px renders. Revisions: stronger ink density; distinct risograph scenes and visible grain; readable interface copy; pixel ending no longer crosses its title; quantized four-colour canvas; opening codex/terminal retain a seed motif. Detector passes with a documented single cream-palette exception mandated by the guide. Renderer is original simplified procedural animation, not physical fluid/paper simulation or full production music arrangement. No copied source artwork.
