How this was made

This original, synthetic gallery follows Claude Opus 5.5 Motion Videos Tutorial by Janus Tiu. His seven palettes, motion languages, prompt structure and review workflow are the starting point. The seed’s journey, every frame and every sound here are new work. No guide pages or reference frames are reproduced.

We replaced the guide’s paid Claude Code / Opus 5.5 setup with locally authored Canvas and JavaScript. Each film is a self-contained HTML file with a deterministic seek(t) function. Seeded variation, storyboard review, frame contact sheets, Playwright and ffmpeg take it from code to a 1080 × 1920, 30 fps film. Music and effects are synthesized in Python and normalized toward −14 LUFS; no sampled music or paid services.

The adaptations follow the seed rather than the guide’s original “MOTION” wordmark. Ink uses a deterministic analytic curl-flow approximation with target attraction, not a full fluid solver; translucent pigment is composited on white rather than adding light directly to white. Loops restart as short films, not seamless cycles.

Motion pauses offscreen. Reduced-motion visitors see a poster until they choose Play. Sound is always optional. The frame counter and playback controls belong to the gallery, outside the reel-safe composition.
