# Seven motions — storyboard approval gate

A seed travels across the world. Seven full-screen rooms tell that same journey through seven motion languages. Original artwork; no source PDF pages or frames will ship.

## Source read
Janus Tiu, **Claude Opus 5.5 Motion Videos Tutorial**, all 14 PDF pages downloaded and text-extracted with pypdf. Source: https://raj-microsoft.github.io/ai-web-design-library/assets/janustiu-claude-motion-videos-tutorial.pdf

**Approved before implementation on 2026-11-09 by the coordinating session under delegated creative direction.** Page 5 requires the beat map and four PNG stills per film, then a stop for approval before building; that gate was honored. These are rough authored storyboard images, not final rendered frames. Review requested distinct risograph phases, a recurring seed, compact MP4s and opt-in sound; those revisions informed the finished films. See each film's contact sheet for the implemented result.

## Common technical contract, adapted from the guide's base block
Each film: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 8 or 9 seconds. One self-contained HTML per film; canvas frames generated by a deterministic `seek(t)` function, seeded randomness, no timer-driven scene logic or CSS transitions. Playwright frame capture plus ffmpeg H.264 MP4 export. Synthesized music and effects, hits aligned to the visual beats, normalized to -14 LUFS and muxed. Four pre-build stills, post-render beat contact sheet and timestamp-specific fixes. Text remains below y=250 and above y=1520, with minimum 60 px main text. No crossfades, generic gradients or glow spam. Web playback has pause/replay and reduced-motion stills; sound starts only on user request. Exported films are linear short films, not falsely advertised as seamless loops.

## Setup substitution
Paid Claude Code / Opus 5.5 is replaced by this code-authoring session, local Python, Canvas/JavaScript, Playwright and installed ffmpeg. No paid services, account creation, API keys or external visual/audio assets. One folder per film is retained. The final gallery will disclose this substitution and prominently credit and link Janus Tiu's guide. Impeccable context setup has run; product/design facts are inferred only from the explicit delegated brief, not from an invented user interview.

## 1. One-line graph — 8 seconds
Palette: paper #F4F1EA; grid #DAD5CA; single stroke #2F5BFF.
- 0–1.5: a climbing wind graph carries one seed-shaped loop.
- 1.5–3: the graph unravels into a continuous orbit of the earth.
- 3–4.5: the same path draws rolling sea and mountain horizons.
- 4.5–6: the travelling stroke spirals down into soil.
- 6–8: it rises into a seedling and a connected handwritten ending, “grow”.
Motion: one never-lifted blue path, even pen velocity with quicker straight runs; no cuts. Sound: 110 BPM marimba and plucked bass, speed-linked scratch, completion plucks.
Fix to verify: every frame remains one unbroken path, not disconnected reveals.
Four keys: `one-line/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `one-line-board.png`.

## 2. One-ball showreel — 8 seconds
Palette: #111116, #EFEBE4, #FF3319, #2A42FF.
- 0–1.2: a red seed-ball drops with three weighted squash-and-stretch bounces.
- 1.2–2.6: it expands to a red field; “GO” snaps across a citylike typographic composition.
- 2.6–4: on cream, orbiting dots become a world around the ever-present ball.
- 4–5.2: a maplike grid flips into red, white and cobalt diamonds.
- 5.2–6.4: segmented rings become a tunnel, carrying the seed through.
- 6.4–8: the pieces lock into “GROW”, the ball becoming its full stop.
Motion: 2–4 frame snaps, short holds, hard beat cuts. Sound: 128 BPM electronic beat, bounce thuds, letter clicks, slice, tunnel riser, terminal bass hit.
Fix: retain the same red seed-ball in every shot.
Keys: `one-ball/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `one-ball-board.png`.

## 3. ASCII terminal — 8 seconds
Palette: #000000 and #33FF66. All visible film marks are monospace glyphs in a rigid grid, including interface labels.
- 0–1: `$ seed --destination earth` types into the terminal.
- 1–3: a lit, rotating 3D seed built from brightness-mapped characters.
- 3–4.5: the seed becomes an orbital globe/torus, still rotating.
- 4.5–5.5: a 2–3 frame row-shift glitch reveals a horizon and landing coordinates.
- 5.5–7: falling characters regroup into an ASCII seedling and “GROW”.
- 7–8: `[ life: online ]` types beneath and holds.
Motion: continuous 3D inside an approximately 90-column grid. Sound: 124 BPM minimal techno, keyboard clicks, low hum, glitch chirps and falling-letter ticks.
Fix: raise grid to 120 columns if the form lacks definition; never substitute raster imagery.
Keys: `ascii/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `ascii-board.png`.

## 4. Pixel dither — 8 seconds
Strict palette: #0F380F, #306230, #8BAC0F, #9BBC0F.
- 0–1.2: a high-resolution floating seed/sphere.
- 1.2–2.4: deliberate resolution steps collapse to a 135×240 field.
- 2.4–4: seed-ball bounces across parallax hills with 2×2 dust.
- 4–5.2: pixel-sort wind smears briefly tear the landscape, then snap clean.
- 5.2–6.6: the seed disperses into pixels and reforms into a seedling and “GROW”.
- 6.6–8: “PRESS GROW” blinks beneath the final form.
Motion: quantized on twos inside 30 fps. Ordered Bayer dithering and nearest-neighbour scaling; no anti-aliasing after the collapse. Sound: 140 BPM square/triangle chiptune, resolution blips, bounce boings, bitcrushed wind and rising arpeggio.
Fix: inspect strict palette and pixel grid, no smoothed final text.
Keys: `pixel/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `pixel-board.png`.

## 5. Risograph cutout — 9 seconds
Palette: #F3EFE6 paper; #FF48B0 and #00838A inks plus overprint.
- 0–1.5: ragged paper seed, cloud, sun and wave slide onto the desk.
- 1.5–3: the pieces fold into a boat carrying the seed.
- 3–4.5: a flap opens into an ocean; a paper route unrolls.
- 4.5–6: the route accordion-folds into mountain and field panels.
- 6–7.5: seed lands, panels stand into cut-paper “GROW”.
- 7.5–9: a leaf pops up; scraps settle; a small “FOUND A HOME” stamp lands.
Motion: on threes, short shadows, tiny deterministic per-frame jitter and landing squash. Fibres, ink grain, halftones and 3px ink misregistration. Sound: 100 BPM toy piano, bass and brushed snare with paper slides, flap whoosh, fold squeeze and stamp.
Fix: increase misregistration and jitter if too clean; preserve two-ink limit.
Keys: `risograph/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `risograph-board.png`.

## 6. Renaissance codex — 9 seconds
Palette: #E9D9B5 parchment; #5A3A1E ink; #A4452C construction chalk.
- 0–1.5: red geometry measures the wind around an empty seed-sized space.
- 1.5–3.5: an original winged seed carriage is drawn: construction, outline, then hatching.
- 3.5–4.5: unreadable mirrored notes, gear and pulley studies fill margins.
- 4.5–6.5: gears turn and the seed's wings flap; the machine lifts, shadow on paper.
- 6.5–9: a fast flight clears the page, curled page-turn reveals “grow” with a rootlike flourish.
Motion: natural stroke pauses and one fast flight. No real historical page copied. Sound: slow lute/harpsichord, quill scratches, wooden gear creaks, wing flaps and page rustle.
Fix: inspect line order and hatch density; avoid digital gradients and glows.
Keys: `codex/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `codex-board.png`.

## 7. Ink in water — 9 seconds
Palette: #FAFAFA, #1B1F6B, #C2185B and violet overlap.
- 0–1: a seedlike indigo drop meets the water surface.
- 1–3.5: roughly 30,000 particles fall and bloom into a mushroom cloud.
- 3.5–4.5: a magenta current joins and curls around the first.
- 4.5–7: a curl-noise field settles particles into “GROW”, with rootlike tendrils.
- 7–9: word holds one second, then its lower edge dissolves into travelling particles.
Motion: slow, organic and seeded; gravity, drag and curl turbulence; gradual sampled-letter target attraction. Sound: ambient piano, no drums, underwater drop plunks, muffled bloom rumble and long swell/tone.
Fix: increase settled-particle fraction if ending word does not read. Proposed compositing clarification: additive coloured ink on a transparent/dark layer composited over white; additive light directly on white would wash out.
Keys: `ink/key-1.png` through `key-4.png`; board `ink-board.png`.
